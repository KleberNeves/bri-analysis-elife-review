# Figure S10. Original-study standard deviation versus mean

Natural-log SD is plotted against natural-log mean separately for EPM, MTT, and PCR. Each point represents a control or treated arm; lines show ordinary least-squares fits and shading indicates 95% prediction intervals. Color identifies the study arm. Shape distinguishes reported SD (SD), SD derived from SEM and reported sample size (SEM (exact)), and SD derived from SEM using the midpoint of a reported sample-size range (SEM (estimated)).

MTT percentage-scale means and SDs were divided by 100. "Treated (Normalized)" denotes treated arms whose normalized control had mean = 1 and SD = 0. The corresponding controls from MTT86, MTT87, MTT96, PCR16, PCR69, PCR147, PCR175, and PCR184 were omitted because ln(SD) is undefined. Their treated arms remain included.
